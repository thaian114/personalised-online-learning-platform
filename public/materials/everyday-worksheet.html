Everyday English

A1 practice worksheet - Units 1-3. Original teaching material by Group 1, CO3103, semester 261.

1. Start and close a conversation

Work with a partner. Greet them, ask how they are, respond and end the conversation politely.

2. Introduce yourself

Write 40 words about your name, hometown and one hobby. Ask your partner two questions.

3. Your daily routine

  1. She ___ (study) English every evening.
  2. I have breakfast ___ seven.
  3. Put in order: finally / first / then.

Describe your morning in five sentences. Include two clock times.

Answer key and speaking checklist

studies; at; first, then, finally.

Example: First, I get up at seven. Then I have breakfast at seven thirty. I walk to university. I study English in the morning. Finally, I have lunch with my friends.

Check: polite opening, hometown, hobby, follow-up question and polite closing. Give one point for each element. This practice task is not an official CEFR assessment.
