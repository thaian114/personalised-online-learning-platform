English Foundations

A1 practice worksheet - Units 1-3. Original teaching material by Group 1, CO3103, semester 261.

1. Greetings

Write a four-line conversation between two students meeting for the first time. Include both names and a polite response.

2. Subject pronouns

  1. Anna is my teacher. ___ works here.
  2. Bao and I are students. ___ study English.
  3. The books are new. ___ are on the desk.

3. The verb be

  1. I ___ ready.
  2. They ___ not tired.
  3. Change into a question: You are a student.

Writing task

Introduce yourself in 40-60 words. Include your name, a role and an interest. Check capital letters and the verb be.

Answer key and self-check

Pronouns: She; We; They. Verb be: am; are; Are you a student?

Sample conversation: Hello, my name is Bao. / Hi, I am Anna. / Nice to meet you, Anna. / Nice to meet you too.

Writing self-check: name included (1), role included (1), interest included (1), correct be forms (1), clear punctuation (1). This is a practice checklist, not a validated proficiency score.
